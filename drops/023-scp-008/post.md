# SCP-008 — Zombie Plague

- Scheduled: 2026-12-06 19:07 America/New_York
- Platforms: Instagram Reel, YouTube Short

## Instagram caption

100% infectious. 100% lethal. You are SCP-008.

Who you are: a prion stored in sealed vaults. You spread through any bodily fluid. Within three hours: fever, then dementia. Around 20 hours later: coma, which counts as death. Then the body rots like gangrene and stands back up, slow-blooded enough that bullet wounds barely bleed. If you get out, the sites that hold you don't evacuate. They incinerate and irradiate.
