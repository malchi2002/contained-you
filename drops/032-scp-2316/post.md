## Instagram caption

SCP-2316 · Field Trip | You do not recognize the bodies in the water. You are SCP-2316.

Who you are: a group of corpses floating in a quarantined lake, somewhere between 45 and 200 of them. Anyone who sees you believes the faces belong to people they knew as kids. Touch the water and more of you surface, and the pull gets stronger. Nobody who walks in comes back. The file keeps glitching with notes from someone who says you were their classmates: a Kirk Lonwood High School field trip, fall 1975. The Foundation watched, they say. Can't you hear them?
