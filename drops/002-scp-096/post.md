# SCP-096 — The Shy Guy

- Scheduled: 2026-11-26 12:07 America/New_York
- Platforms: Instagram Reel, YouTube Short

## Instagram caption

Someone saw your face. Now you have to find them. You are SCP-096.

Who you are: a pale, 2.38-metre humanoid with arms far too long for your body, kept in an airtight steel cube with no cameras allowed. If anyone sees your face, in person, in a photo or on video, you cover your face, scream and cry, then run straight to them. Drawings don't count. Over a thousand rounds didn't slow you down. A bag over your head did.
