# SCP-096 — The Shy Guy

- Scheduled: 2026-11-26 12:07 America/New_York

## Instagram caption

Someone saw your face. Now you have to find them. You are SCP-096.

Who you are: a tall, pale, quiet thing that spends its days crying in a sealed steel cube. You are harmless, until someone sees your face. A photo, a video, four pixels of you: it doesn't matter. Once they've seen you, you will run across anything to reach them, and nothing they own can stop you.
