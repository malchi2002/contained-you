# SCP-1440 — The Old Man from Nowhere

- Scheduled: 2026-12-09 19:07 America/New_York
- Platforms: Instagram Reel, YouTube Short

## Instagram caption

Everything human falls apart near you. You are SCP-1440.

Who you are: an old man who hasn't aged in fifty years, carrying a sack, a worn deck of cards and a small glass cup. Stay near people or anything they've made for more than a few days, and it all starts breaking until it's destroyed. You know it, and you try to stay away, but something compels you to keep walking into towns. When they brought you in, the site was destroyed. You asked them to destroy you.
