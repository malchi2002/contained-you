# SCP-372 — Peripheral Jumper

- Scheduled: 2026-12-07 12:07 America/New_York
- Platforms: Instagram Reel, YouTube Short

## Instagram caption

It's in the corner of your eye. You are SCP-372.

Who you are: a two-metre, eight-legged creature that senses brain activity and times its movements to it. You hide behind people's heads, in their blind spots and in the instant their eyes move. You once followed a patient everywhere, staying in his sight and out of everyone else's, until they called it hallucinations. If you get out, staff are told to watch the corners of their eyes.
