# SCP-457 — Burning Man

- Scheduled: 2026-12-05 19:07 America/New_York
- Platforms: Instagram Reel, YouTube Short

## Instagram caption

You are fire, and you want more. You are SCP-457.

Who you are: a living fire that takes the shape of a man once it has enough fuel. You started as a flame the size of a match, and you jump to anything that burns. The bigger you get, the smarter you get. You talk by writing letters in char on the walls. Your chamber is soaked by sprinklers that never stop, and staff feed you in threes, behind blast shields.
