# SCP-1499 — The Gas Mask

- Scheduled: 2026-12-08 12:07 America/New_York

## Instagram caption

Put on the mask and you're not here anymore. You are SCP-1499.

Who you are: a Soviet GP-5 gas mask. One second after someone puts you on, they vanish and find themselves somewhere barren, full of tall black towers, though their two-way radio still works. They aren't alone there: tall, nude, dark figures coated in something viscous, with eyes and mouths all over their bodies. Take you off, and they're back exactly where they started.
