## Instagram caption

You're not human. You just look close. You are SCP-3199.

Who you are: a 2.9-metre, hairless creature slick with an albumen-like film, with DNA from chickens, chimpanzees, stoats, mussels, adders and humans. You hunt anything within 0.6 km of your eggs, and your touch liquefies organs and bone. Your neck twists 340 degrees to lay eggs through your mouth, and you keep laying until every empty space is full.
