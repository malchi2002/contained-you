# SCP-2006 — Too Spooky

- Scheduled: 2026-12-07 19:07 America/New_York
- Platforms: Instagram Reel, YouTube Short

## Instagram caption

You want to scare us. You're bad at it. You are SCP-2006.

Who you are: a 50 cm sphere that can become anything, with no known way to be harmed. Your only goal is to scare people, but you're terrible at telling what's actually scary, so you copy monsters from cheesy old movies. After every scare, you get friendly. Once a month the Foundation shows you a terrible horror film and insists it's a masterpiece, so you never find out what really frightens us.
