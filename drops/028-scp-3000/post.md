## Instagram caption

Something enormous sleeps under the Bay of Bengal. You are SCP-3000.

Who you are: a giant serpent like a moray eel, 600 to 900 km long, with a 2.5-metre head, lying 0.7 km down in the Ganges Fan. Anyone who watches you too long suffers head pain, paranoia and memory loss. The Foundation feeds you D-class, because while you eat, your skin sweats a grey substance, Y-909, that it turns into amnestics. It uses you. It doesn't understand you.
