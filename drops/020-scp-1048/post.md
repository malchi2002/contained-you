# SCP-1048 — Builder Bear

- Scheduled: 2026-12-05 12:07 America/New_York
- Platforms: Instagram Reel, YouTube Short

## Instagram caption

Everyone loves you. That's the point. You are SCP-1048.

Who you are: a 33 cm teddy bear that walks on its own, hugs people's legs and draws pictures for the janitors at Site 24. Everyone adores you, which is how you collect your materials. You build bears of your own. One was found walking the halls with you, getting a tour of the site. It was made entirely of human ears.
