# SCP-513 — A Cowbell

- Scheduled: 2026-12-08 19:07 America/New_York
- Platforms: Instagram Reel, YouTube Short

## Instagram caption

Don't ring it. Something answers. You are SCP-513.

Who you are: a rusty cowbell, sealed in a cube of gelatin. Anyone who hears you ring feels watched. An hour later they start glimpsing something in doorways and mirrors, and it turns and runs. Every 14 to 237 minutes it comes back, so they stop sleeping. If they do fall asleep, it attacks them. Only surgically deafened staff handle you now.
