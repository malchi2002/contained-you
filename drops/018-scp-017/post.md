# SCP-017 — Shadow Person

- Scheduled: 2026-12-04 12:07 America/New_York

## Instagram caption

Don't cast a shadow on it. You are SCP-017.

Who you are: an 80 cm figure made of shadow and smoke. Cast a shadow on you and you leap, wrap the thing completely, shrink back to normal size and leave no trace of it behind. Your cage is lit by arc lamps from every angle, staff wear reflective suits, and a backup generator is always ready for when a lamp burns out.
