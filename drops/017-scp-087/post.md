# SCP-087 — The Stairwell

- Scheduled: 2026-12-03 19:07 America/New_York
- Platforms: Instagram Reel, YouTube Short

## Instagram caption

The stairs go down. They don't stop. You are SCP-087.

Who you are: an unlit stairwell behind a door at Site-19. Thirteen steps per flight, 38 degrees, turning at every platform, and no light over 75 watts makes it brighter. A voice cries from far below, and no one ever gets closer to it. Every explorer meets a face with no pupils, no nose and no mouth. After the fourth exploration, no one is allowed down.
